Hover a phrase for its gloss — transliteration, vocabulary, meaning, the way the reading editions set them. Click a sentence to replay the video from its beginning; the greyed lines around the spoken one are its context and stay hoverable too. Shift-click a phrase to copy it (the whole caption, if you shift-click beside one; the cloud has a copy button too). Alt-click (or Ctrl-click) a word to turn the text into a card — or use the “+ card” button inside any gloss cloud for the whole phrase. The card goes to an Anki deck, to one of your exercise decks, or onto the clipboard as markdown for a studio document. It can carry the word's own sound, cut with “🔊 cut the audio…”: from the film, for a film on this machine; from a recording of this tab, for a YouTube video in Chrome or Edge. The cloud is where a phrase is written too: the four dots mark it in the reading editions' own colours, and ✎ edit opens its fields — the text, the transliteration, the vocabulary, the meaning, the note — and saves them into this video's annotations.
