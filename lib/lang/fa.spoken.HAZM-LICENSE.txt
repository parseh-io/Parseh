Hazm's list of informal Persian words (iwords.dat) seeded lib/lang/fa.spoken.words.tsv, and its list of informal
verbs (iverbs.dat) lib/lang/fa.spoken.verbs.tsv: the rows whose `src` column says `hazm` (or `note+hazm`) are
entries of those lists, checked one by one, in Parseh's own format.  Hazm is
https://github.com/roshan-research/hazm and is under the licence below, which asks that it travel with the copies.
